# Gonads — steroidogenic cell packing (hover note)

**Region:** Testis (Leydig), ovary (theca / luteal) — steroid-making cells

**Physics to surface:** Dense smooth ER and lipid droplets pack the cytoplasm — extreme excluded volume and short diffusion paths for lipid-soluble intermediates. Membrane area per volume is enormous; transport is often membrane-adjacent, not open-cytoplasm.

**One-liner for map:** Hormone factories are packed with membrane folds — crowding and tiny travel distances speed local chemistry.

**Sex layers:** male + female (parallel). Hormone pathways detail → BioChem / Receptors; physics packing only here.
