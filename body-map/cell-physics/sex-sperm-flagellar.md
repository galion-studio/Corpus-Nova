# Male — sperm (flagellar / low-Re physics) hover note

**Region:** Testis / male reproductive tract (spermatozoa)

**Physics to surface:** Sperm swim at very low Reynolds number — friction rules, no coasting (Purcell). Flagellar beat is a driven bending wave; crowding and viscosity of cervical mucus change how far the wave carries.

**One-liner for map:** Sperm are tiny swimmers in tar-like physics — flagella push because coasting is impossible at that size.
