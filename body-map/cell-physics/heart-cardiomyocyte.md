# Heart — cardiomyocyte physics (hover note)

**Region:** Heart / cardiac muscle cells

**Physics to surface:** Force generation by actin–myosin (piconewton-scale motors), calcium-triggered contraction timing, and a crowded sarcomere lattice where spacing matters.

**One-liner for map:** Heart cells are force machines: molecular motors pull in piconewtons inside a tightly packed filament lattice.

**Library:** `library/cell-physics/force-energy-scales.md`

**Sourced numbers:** single myosin pulls ~3–4 pN (Finer et al. 1994); heat energy k_BT ≈ 4.3 pN·nm at 37 °C. See `sources/cell-physics-01-sources.md` #5.
