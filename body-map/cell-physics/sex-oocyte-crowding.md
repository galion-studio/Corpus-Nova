# Female — oocyte (size & crowding) hover note

**Region:** Ovary / oocyte

**Physics to surface:** Oocytes are giant cells — diffusion times grow with distance squared, so they rely on localized stores, motors, and gradients more than small somatic cells. Cytoplasmic crowding and large organelles still shape movement and condensate-like assemblies.

**One-liner for map:** Egg cells are huge on cell scales — physics makes long-distance diffusion too slow, so organization beats random mixing.
