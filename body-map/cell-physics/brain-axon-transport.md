# Brain / nerves — axon crowding & transport (hover note)

**Region:** Brain, peripheral nerves (axon)

**Physics to surface:** Long-distance transport along microtubules; crowding and cargo size limit how motors move; anomalous diffusion in crowded cytosol of the axon.

**One-liner for map:** Axons are highways under crowding — motors walk tracks while cargo fights a packed interior.

**Library:** `library/cell-physics/macromolecular-crowding.md`, `anomalous-diffusion.md`

**Sourced numbers:** kinesin stalls ~5.5–7 pN with 8 nm steps (Visscher et al. 1999). Plain diffusion down a 1 m axon would take centuries (our estimate from Swaminathan et al. 1997) — motors are essential. See `sources/cell-physics-01-sources.md` #6, #7.
