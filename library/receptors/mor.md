# µ-opioid receptor (MOR / OPRM1)

**Plain idea:** MOR is a social-reward dial. Warm contact can feel good through this door; exclusion can feel like pain when the same systems run in reverse.

**Exact name:** µ-opioid receptor · gene `OPRM1` · Gi/o-coupled GPCR.

**Where it shows up:** Brain reward and pain-overlap circuits (incl. regions tied to social loss); also classic opioid pharmacology sites.

**Why connection cares:** Affiliative touch and social rejection both brush MOR-linked pathways — “connection” and “hurt” share hardware.
