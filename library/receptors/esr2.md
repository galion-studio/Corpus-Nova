# Estrogen receptor β (ERβ)

**Plain idea:** ERβ is estrogen's second nuclear lock. It was found late (1996), in the prostate and ovary of the rat. It often acts as a brake or a fine-tuner next to ERα.

**Exact name:** Estrogen receptor beta · gene `ESR2` · nuclear receptor NR3A2 · ligand: 17β-estradiol (binds it tightly; K_d ≈ 0.6 nM in the original cloning paper).

**How it works:** Same basic plan as ERα: DNA-binding domain + ligand-binding domain → binds EREs → changes gene reading. It can pair with ERα (a heterodimer — a mixed pair) and change ERα's output.

**Where it shows up:**
- Female: ovarian granulosa cells (strongly), brain
- Male: prostate secretory epithelium, testis, brain
- Both: many tissues at lower levels

**Why it matters for the map:** In the granulosa cells that grow each follicle, ERβ is the leading estrogen receptor. In the prostate it sits in the gland lining, while ERα and AR dominate the surrounding tissue.

**Body-map tags:** ovary.follicle.granulosa, prostate
