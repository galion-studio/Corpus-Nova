# FSH receptor (FSHR)

**Plain idea:** FSHR is where follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) lands. In ovaries it grows follicles and turns androgens into estrogen. In testes it keeps the sperm-nursing Sertoli cells working.

**Exact name:** Follicle-stimulating hormone receptor · gene `FSHR` · class A GPCR (glycoprotein-hormone receptor family, cousin of LHCGR) · ligand: FSH · main signal: Gs → cAMP → PKA.

**Where it shows up:**
- Female: granulosa cells
- Male: Sertoli cells
- Essentially only in the gonads (claims of FSHR elsewhere remain debated)

**Two-cell, two-hormone trick (ovary):** Theca cells use LH to make androgens. Granulosa cells use FSH to switch on aromatase, which turns those androgens into estradiol. Two cells, two receptors, one estrogen.

**Body-map tags:** ovary.follicle.granulosa, testis.sertoli

**Key sources:** Simoni, Gromoll & Nieschlag 1997 · Richards & Pangas 2010
