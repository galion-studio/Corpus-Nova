# Vasopressin receptor 1A (AVPR1A)

**Plain idea:** AVPR1A is one of vasopressin’s social ears — especially loud in recognition and pair-bond stories across mammals (with careful human translation).

**Exact name:** Arginine vasopressin receptor 1A · gene `AVPR1A` · Gq-coupled GPCR.

**Where it shows up:** CNS circuits for social memory and affiliation; expression patterns differ by species and sex — always check the map before generalizing.

**Why connection cares:** Promoter variants (classic RS3 length stories in the literature) are research hooks for individual differences in social behavior — correlation ≠ destiny.
