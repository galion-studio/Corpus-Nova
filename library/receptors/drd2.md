# Dopamine D2 receptor (D2)

**Plain idea:** D2 is part of dopamine's "do that again" system. Paired with D1, it helps turn a good social moment into a habit of seeking that person out.

**Exact name:** Dopamine receptor D2 · gene `DRD2` · class A GPCR · Gi/o (lowers cAMP) · close cousin D3 (`DRD3`).

**Where it shows up (connection-relevant):** nucleus accumbens, dorsal striatum, prefrontal cortex; also the pituitary, where it holds prolactin back.

**Why connection cares:** In prairie voles, D2-type signalling in the nucleus accumbens is needed to *form* a pair bond. Later, D1-type signalling helps *keep* it, by making the vole reject new partners (Aragona et al. 2006). Measured in living humans with [¹¹C]raclopride PET.

**Key sources:** Aragona et al. 2006 · Young & Wang 2004
