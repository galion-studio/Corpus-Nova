# Oxytocin receptor (OXTR)

**Plain idea:** OXTR is the door oxytocin knocks on. When it opens, cells shift toward trust, care, and bonding — but only where the door exists, and only as loud as the local wiring allows.

**Exact name:** Oxytocin receptor · gene `OXTR` · class A GPCR · mainly Gq/11 → PLC → IP₃/Ca²⁺.

**Where it shows up (connection-relevant):**
- Brain: hypothalamus (PVN links), nucleus accumbens, amygdala, some prefrontal areas
- Body: uterus, mammary tissue, heart and vessels (peripheral “feel” of connection states)

**Why connection cares:** Ligand (oxytocin) + OXTR density + gene variants (e.g. rs53576 in research literature) reshape how social cues land. Same hug, different receptor setup → different inner story.

**Body-map tags:** brain.pvn, brain.nacc, brain.amygdala, heart, uterus
