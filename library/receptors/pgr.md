# Progesterone receptor (PR)

**Plain idea:** PR is the "get ready and stay calm" lock. Progesterone through PR gets the uterine lining ready for an embryo, keeps the uterine muscle quiet in pregnancy, and branches the breast.

**Exact name:** Progesterone receptor · gene `PGR` · nuclear receptor NR3C3 · ligand: progesterone · **two isoforms** (two versions made from one gene): PR-A (shorter) and PR-B (longer).

**How it works:** Genomic nuclear-receptor action, like AR. Estrogen through ERα switches on the PGR gene, so the estrogen phase builds the PR that the progesterone phase then uses.

**Who does what (mouse knockouts):**
- **PR-A:** needed for ovulation and uterine readiness. Without PR-A, the ovary and uterus fail.
- **PR-B:** needed for breast side-branching and milk lobules, partly through the RANKL signal. Without PR-B, the ovary and uterus are fine but the breast under-branches.

**Where it shows up:** endometrium, myometrium, cervix, fallopian tube, breast, granulosa cells after the LH surge, brain (hypothalamus); lower levels in male tissues.
