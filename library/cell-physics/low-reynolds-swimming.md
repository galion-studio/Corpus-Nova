# Low Reynolds number swimming

**What it is:** When you are tiny and in water, friction beats momentum. Stop pushing and you stop almost at once. The Reynolds number compares the two; for cells and sperm it is far below 1.

**Why it matters:** Sperm flagella and many micro-swimmers need continuous driven motion — reciprocal “sculling” tricks that work for big animals fail here (Purcell’s scallop theorem).

**Key scales:** Micrometers; viscosity of water ~1 mPa·s; speeds of tens to hundreds of µm/s for sperm in fluid (medium-dependent).
