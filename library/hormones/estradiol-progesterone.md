# Estradiol & progesterone: chemistry and the cycle loop

**One-liner:** The ovary makes estrogen by converting androgen. Its control loop flips from "brake" to "accelerator" once a month to trigger ovulation.

**Chemistry**
- **Estradiol (E2)** is an 18-carbon steroid with an aromatic first ring, made by **aromatase (CYP19A1)** from testosterone. Estrone (E1) is made from androstenedione and becomes E2 via 17β-HSD1.
- **Progesterone** is a 21-carbon steroid one step past the gate (pregnenolone → 3β-HSD2). The corpus luteum is built to stop the line here.

**Daily ovarian output across the cycle** (Baird & Fraser 1974)

| | Early follicular | Pre-ovulation | Mid-luteal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Estradiol | ~36 µg | ~380 µg | ~250 µg |
| Progesterone | ~1 mg | ~4 mg | ~25 mg |

**Typical blood levels** (these depend on the assay, so check lab ranges):
- E2 runs from tens of pg/mL early in the cycle to a few hundred pg/mL before ovulation.
- Progesterone is below ~1 ng/mL before ovulation and around 10–20 ng/mL at mid-luteal.

**The loop**
1. For most of the cycle, E2 and progesterone **damp** GnRH, LH, and FSH (negative feedback).
2. When E2 stays above **~200 pg/mL for ~50 h**, the loop flips to **positive feedback**, which triggers the **LH surge**.
3. Ovulation follows **~34–36 h** after the surge begins.
4. The corpus luteum makes progesterone for **~14 days**. Without hCG from a pregnancy it shuts down, hormones fall, menstruation starts, FSH rises, and the cycle repeats.

**In men:** estradiol is low but essential. About 80% is made outside the testis, mostly by fat aromatase, and it helps control body fat (Finkelstein et al. 2013) and bone.
**After menopause:** ovarian E2 stops, and estrone from fat aromatase becomes the main estrogen.

**Carriage:** E2 rides about 78% on albumin, ~20% on SHBG, and ~2% free.
