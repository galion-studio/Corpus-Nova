# Estrogen & progesterone — overview card

**One-liner:** estradiol is the "build and prepare" signal, progesterone the "hold and protect" signal — and the ovary hands off between them every cycle.

Map: `pelvis.ovary`, `pelvis.uterus`, `thorax.breast` (female_layer) · aromatase note on `pelvis.testis` and fat depots (male_layer, shared).

## Estrogens
- **Main forms:** estradiol (E2, the strong one in reproductive years), estrone (E1, dominant after menopause, made mostly in fat), estriol (E3, pregnancy/placenta).
- **Made by:** **aromatase (CYP19A1)**, which turns androgens into estrogens — androstenedione → estrone, testosterone → estradiol [19, 20].
- **Ovarian "two-cell, two-gonadotropin" model:** **theca cells** (LH-driven) make androgens; neighbouring **granulosa cells** (FSH-driven) aromatize them to estradiol [7, 19]. Neither cell type can do the whole job alone — a two-person relay.
- **Men:** estradiol is made mostly by aromatizing testosterone outside the testis (fat, bone, brain); it is required for male bone health and feedback on LH [19].
- **Acts via:** ESR1 (ERα), ESR2 (ERβ) nuclear receptors and the membrane receptor GPER → **Receptors** theme.

## Progesterone
- **Made by:** the **corpus luteum** after ovulation (and later the placenta); small amounts from adrenals and, in men, as an intermediate in steroid synthesis [7, 19].
- **Job:** turns the estrogen-built endometrium into a *secretory*, implantation-ready lining; thickens cervical mucus; slightly raises basal body temperature (the dip-and-rise used by cycle-tracking studies [24]).
- If no pregnancy: the corpus luteum fades after ~12 days, progesterone falls, the lining sheds — menstruation [7, 24].
- **Acts via:** PGR (PR-A / PR-B) → **Receptors** theme.

## Lifetime arc (♀)
| Stage | Estradiol / progesterone pattern |
|---|---|
| Childhood | low; HPG axis quiet |
| Puberty | GnRH pulses wake up → rising E2 → breast, uterine growth, menarche |
| Reproductive years | monthly cycle: E2 peak before ovulation, progesterone peak in luteal phase (see `library/female-reproductive/cycle.md`) |
| Pregnancy | placenta takes over; very high E2/E3 and progesterone |
| Menopause (~50–51 y) | follicle supply runs out [27]; E2 and progesterone fall; FSH rises; fat distribution often shifts central (→ Adipose) |
