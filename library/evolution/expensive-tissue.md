# Expensive tissue — and the fat update

**Claim:** A large brain is metabolically costly. Classic story: humans “paid” for it by shrinking the gut (high-quality diet → smaller GI → spare energy for neurons). Comparative mammal work later found **no** general brain↔gut mass trade-off once fat-free mass is controlled — but a brain↔**adipose** trade-off: big brains tend to pair with leaner stores.

**Why it stops you:** The popular “small gut funded the brain” line is incomplete; the safer energy archive may be the real bargaining chip across mammals, while the human gut story still matters for *diet quality* and map anatomy.

**Evidence:** Aiello & Wheeler 1995 (*Curr Anthropol* 36:199–221, doi:10.1086/204350). Navarrete, van Schaik & Isler 2011 (*Nature* 480:91–94, PMID 22080949).

**Confidence:** High for “brain is expensive + diet quality matters”; medium for exact organ-vs-organ bookkeeping (ETH revised).

**Map:** abdomen (gut length/ferment), head (brain cost), adipose depots (Adipose theme).
