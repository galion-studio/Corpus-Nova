# Constrained total energy expenditure

**Claim:** After body size/composition, daily energy burn rises with activity at low levels, then **plateaus**. The body compensates — it does not keep adding calories 1:1 as you move more.

**Why it stops you:** “Just exercise more to create a bigger calorie sink” fights physiology. Hunter-gatherer and industrial samples can show similar total expenditure once size is matched.

**Evidence:** Pontzer et al. 2016 (*Curr Biol* 26:410–417, PMID 26832439, doi:10.1016/j.cub.2015.12.046). Doubly labeled water + accelerometry, n=332, five populations.

**Confidence:** High for the plateau pattern in that design; ongoing debate on mechanisms and public-health translation.
