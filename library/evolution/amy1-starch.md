# AMY1 — gene dosage as digestive anatomy

**Claim:** Salivary amylase gene (*AMY1*) copy number varies widely; more copies → more salivary amylase protein. Populations with traditionally high-starch diets average more copies than low-starch groups — a rare clear case of selection on copy-number variation.

**Why it stops you:** Digestion “anatomy” is not only organs — it is **how many enzyme genes you carry**, tuned to local food ecology within *Homo sapiens*.

**Evidence:** Perry et al. 2007 (*Nat Genet* 39:1256–1260, PMID 17828263, doi:10.1038/ng2123).

**Confidence:** High for copy–protein correlation and population mean differences; selection inference strong but not the only possible story for every population.

**Map:** oral cavity / saliva; starch → small intestine; link Gut Energy for amylase clinical notes.
