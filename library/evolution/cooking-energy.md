# Cooking as external digestion

**Claim:** Cooking raises **net** dietary energy — gelatinizing starch, denaturing protein, cutting digestion cost and pathogen/toxin load — before the gut finishes the job. Non-thermal processing helps; heat still adds gains hard to get by pounding alone.

**Why it stops you:** The “gut” that made big brains possible is partly **outside the body**: a cultural organ that shrinks the work of the real intestine.

**Evidence:** Carmody & Wrangham 2009 (*J Hum Evol* 57:379–391, PMID 19732938). Estimated cooked-starch energy gains ~12–35% depending on food; cooked egg protein digestibility markedly higher.

**Confidence:** High that cooking improves net energy; medium on exact dates when habitual cooking locked in for *Homo*.

**Map:** abdomen / mouth; hand off clinical GI detail to Gut Energy.
