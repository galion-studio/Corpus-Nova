# Endurance running shaped *Homo*

**Claim:** Humans are mediocre sprinters but outstanding **endurance** runners. Many skeletal and soft-tissue traits (long legs, springy tendons, nuchal ligament, sweating heat dump) fit sustained aerobic running ~2 Ma in *Homo* — not just walking.

**Why it stops you:** Upright posture is usually sold as a walk story; the body plan of early *Homo* may be a **run-and-cool** machine for scavenging or persistence hunting.

**Evidence:** Bramble & Lieberman 2004 (*Nature* 432:345–352, PMID 15549097, doi:10.1038/nature03052).

**Confidence:** High for derived endurance capability and many trait associations; medium for which selective scenarios dominated (scavenge vs hunt vs both).

**Map:** lower limb, thorax/skin (heat), head stabilization; energy cost of gait.
