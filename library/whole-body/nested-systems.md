# Body as nested systems

**Level:** overview · **Audience:** universal · **Links:** white paper `whole-body-anatomy-overview.md`, map `body-map/`

## One idea
Life stacks: molecules build cells, cells build tissues, tissues build organs, organs join systems, systems make one organism.

## Systems (flyover)
- **Integument** — skin and friends; boundary and sensor  
- **Musculoskeletal** — bones and muscles; scaffold and actuators  
- **Nervous** — fast electrical wiring  
- **Cardiovascular + pulmonary** — blood flow and gas exchange  
- **Gastrointestinal** — intake and chemical transformation  
- **Renal + endocrine** — filtration and chemical broadcast  
- **Immune + reproductive** — defense and lineage  

Sibling themes go deeper (cell physics, receptors, bone volumes, adipose, biochem). This entry stays at the map level.
