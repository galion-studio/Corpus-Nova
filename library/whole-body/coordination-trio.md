# Coordination trio

**Nervous × endocrine × immune** — the body’s chat layer.

- **Nervous:** milliseconds; wires and synapses  
- **Endocrine:** minutes to days; hormones in blood  
- **Immune:** local and systemic; cells and signals that defend and clean up  

Together they keep set points (homeostasis) and adapt under load (allostasis). For receptor-level detail, see the Receptors theme and `library/receptors/`.
