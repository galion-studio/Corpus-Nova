# Redox pairs (NAD⁺/NADH & glutathione)

**One-liner:** Cells move electrons the way a city moves packages — with named carriers and careful handoffs.

**In plain words:** NAD⁺ picks up electrons (becomes NADH) during fuel breakdown, then hands them toward oxygen in mitochondria. Glutathione helps mop reactive oxygen leftovers so membranes and proteins aren’t casually damaged.

**Watch-out:** “Antioxidant” headlines oversimplify; the useful story is **controlled** redox, not zero oxidation.
