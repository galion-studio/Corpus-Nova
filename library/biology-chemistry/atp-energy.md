# ATP — the body’s spendable energy coin

**One-liner:** Cells don’t “store electricity”; they charge a small molecule (ATP) and spend it on almost every job.

**In plain words:** Food energy is converted into ATP. Muscle, pumps, and building reactions “pay” by splitting ATP. Creatine phosphate is a quick local backup in muscle.

**Key idea:** Life runs on **rates and replenishment**, not a giant battery. Stop making ATP for minutes in the brain, and chemistry fails fast.
