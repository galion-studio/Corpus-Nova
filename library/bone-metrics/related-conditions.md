# Related conditions (pointers only)

Theme 4 does not own full disease papers. Short links for the universal library:

- **Osteoporosis / low bone mass:** lower BV and thinner cortex — sector matters (spine trabecular vs hip mixed).
- **Fracture risk:** often highest where cortical walls thin and trabecular volume drops (e.g. distal radius, proximal femur). Cortical-site non-vertebral fractures are about 80% of all fractures [Zebaze 2010]. Women's femoral-neck superoposterior cortex thins about 6.4% per decade [Mayhew 2005].
- **Local asymmetry after injury or unloading:** disuse can shrink volume on one side.
- **Systemic diseases** (e.g. some inflammatory or endocrine disorders): may alter bone remodeling globally — see library disease entries owned by other themes when present.

Keep disease deep-dives elsewhere; this file only orients bone-metric readers.
