# White, brown, and beige fat

**White adipocytes** — one big lipid droplet; main job is long-term energy storage and signaling.  
**Brown adipocytes** — many small droplets and lots of mitochondria; main job is turning fuel into heat.  
**Beige adipocytes** — white-like cells that can be recruited toward the heat-making style (for example in cold).

Map tip: brown-fat pockets in adults are mostly upper chest / neck — see `body-map/adipose-regions.md`.
