# Fat depots

A **depot** is a neighborhood of fat tissue.

- **Subcutaneous** — under the skin (hips, thighs, belly wall). Largest store in most people.
- **Visceral** — deeper in the belly, around organs. Smaller volume, stronger ties to metabolic risk in many studies.
- **Specialized** — e.g. epicardial (on the heart), perirenal (near kidneys), bone marrow fat.

Same chemistry of storage; different neighbors, blood flow, and signals.
