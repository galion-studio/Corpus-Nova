# How fat is stored

**Also called:** triacylglycerol (TAG) storage, lipid droplet packing

When you eat more energy than you burn, the leftovers need a home. The body prefers to pack them as **triacylglycerol** — three fatty-acid tails on a glycerol backbone — inside oily blobs called **lipid droplets** inside fat cells (**adipocytes**).

Why this format? It is dense, water-free, and reversible: the same droplet can grow after a feast and shrink when fuel is needed elsewhere.
