# Making more fat tissue

Two ways stored fat volume grows:

1. **Hypertrophy** — existing fat cells get bigger (larger lipid droplets).  
2. **Hyperplasia / adipogenesis** — precursor cells become new adipocytes.

Early in life, making new cells is common. In adults, growth is often mostly hypertrophy, with new-cell recruitment still possible in some depots. Both paths matter for how “full” a depot feels to the rest of the body.
