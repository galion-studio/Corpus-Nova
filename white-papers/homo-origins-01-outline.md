## Sections
1. **Hook — the body as an energy strategy.** Human = high-income, high-spend ape. Thesis: anatomy is the receipt of an energy budget.
2. **The expensive brain and the trade it forced.** Aiello & Wheeler 1995 expensive-tissue hypothesis; Navarrete et al. 2011 refutation of the gut-mass trade + brain↔fat trade; Herculano-Houzel neuron cost; Pontzer et al. 2016 metabolic acceleration (+~400 kcal/day vs *Pan*).
3. **A small, fast gut and the external stomach.** Ape fermentation gut vs human small-intestine gut (Milton & Demment 1988; Milton 1999, 2003); cooking & processing as external digestion (Wrangham & Conklin-Brittain 2003; Carmody & Wrangham 2009; Carmody et al. 2011); feeding time 4.7% vs 48% (Organ et al. 2011); tools + meat cut chewing (Zink & Lieberman 2016); fire evidence ~1.0 Ma (Berna et al. 2012); cooked starch (Hardy et al. 2015). Hand-off → Gut Energy for segment anatomy.
4. **Locomotion: cheap walking, endurance running, heat dump.** Sockol et al. 2007 (walking ~75% cheaper than chimp); Bramble & Lieberman 2004; Pontzer 2017 mosaic endurance suite; Lieberman 2015 heat loss; Kamberov et al. 2018 sweat glands ~10×.
5. **Energy budget rules: constrained, not additive.** Pontzer et al. 2012 (Hadza), 2014 (primate TEE ~50% of mammal expectation), 2016 *Curr Biol* (constrained TEE), 2021 (life-course TEE).
6. **Enzymes and genes: dietary software updates.** *AMY1* copy number (Perry et al. 2007; Usher et al. 2015; Falchi et al. 2014 debate); lactase persistence (Enattah 2002; Bersaglieri 2004; Tishkoff 2007; Itan 2009; Gerbault 2011; Ségurel & Bon 2017; Evershed 2022). Hand-off → Gut Energy for intolerance clinical detail.
7. **The microbiome as a co-evolved energy organ.** VFA ≈ 10% of human energy (Bergman 1990); cospeciation (Moeller et al. 2016); diversity loss (Moeller et al. 2014); Hadza (Schnorr et al. 2014; Smits et al. 2017); diet-induced extinctions (Sonnenburg et al. 2016). Hand-off → Gut Energy for clinical/immune/mood.
8. **Sex biology of the budget (light, equal depth).** Gestation/lactation costs (Butte & King 2005; Dufour & Sauther 2002); metabolic hypothesis for altriciality (Dunsworth et al. 2012); pelvis width ≠ locomotor cost (Warrener et al. 2015); body-composition dimorphism and plasticity (Wells 2007, 2012); infant fat (Cunnane & Crawford 2003; hypothesis-level). Hand-off → Adipose, Sex Biology.
9. **So what + map/library pointers.** Design rules; region table → overlay JSON.
10. **Further reading.**
