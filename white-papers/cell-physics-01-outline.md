## Abstract angle
A living cell is soft-matter physics at tiny scales: crowded, sticky, and never at rest. This paper shows why “empty water” intuitions fail inside cytoplasm — and which scales (length, time, force) replace them.

## Sections
1. **Opening beat** — A molecule’s “random walk” is not the dilute story from a glass of water.
2. **Crowding as soft matter** — Packing fraction, excluded volume, and why a cytoplasm full of proteins changes everything.
3. **Diffusion that misbehaves** — Normal vs anomalous diffusion; wiggly vs trapped paths.
4. **The cytoplasm as a soft gel** — Elasticity + viscosity; why short times and long times feel different.
5. **Meetings under crowding** — How crowding changes how often molecules find each other.
6. **Force and energy scales that matter** — Thermal energy, piconewtons, nanometers — motors and membranes in the same units.
7. **Why your cells care** — Signaling hubs, condensates, transport; brief links to disease-relevant crowding (high level).
8. **Further reading** — Classic + modern sources (verified).
