# Cell Physics series — planned papers (Theme 2)

Voice: universal, easy, fun, accurate. Each paper feeds `library/cell-physics/` and `body-map/cell-physics/` payloads first. Not yet written beyond paper 01.

1. **The Crowded Cell: Why Physics Feels Different Inside** — *(written)* crowding, anomalous diffusion, viscoelastic cytoplasm, kT and pN scales.
2. **Bones of the Cell: How the Cytoskeleton Turns Piconewtons into Shape** — actin, microtubules, and intermediate filaments as polymers; stiffness, tension, and cell mechanics (cardiomyocyte, endothelium hooks).
3. **Walkers on Tracks: Molecular Motors and Long-Distance Delivery** — kinesin, dynein, myosin as Brownian ratchets; axonal transport; why diffusion fails over long distances.
4. **Skin of the Cell: Membrane Bending, Tension, and Squeezing Through Gaps** — lipid bilayer elasticity, curvature, red-cell deformability in capillaries.
5. **Swimming in Syrup: Life at Low Reynolds Number** — flagella, cilia, and sperm swimming mechanics; mucus as a viscoelastic barrier (sex-biology hook: gametes).
6. **Feeling the Push: Mechanosensing and Mechanotransduction** — how cells read force, stiffness, and flow (shear on vessel lining, stretch in heart and gut).
7. **Drops Without Walls: The Physics of Condensates** — phase separation, crowding-tuned condensates, liquid-to-solid aging and disease hooks (high level; chemistry stays with BioChem).

Sex-biology cell-physics hooks to place inside the papers above (not separate reproductive papers): sperm flagellar mechanics (paper 5), oocyte size and cytoplasmic crowding/transport (papers 1 follow-up / 3), steroid-making cells with dense ER packing (paper 2 or 7).
