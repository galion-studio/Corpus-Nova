# Foundations — The beginning of life as an open question

## The principle

The beginning of life is an **open question** our civilization cannot yet answer.

We do not lack only an answer. We do not yet have the **question** that would give us the answer — the precise framing that would make life’s origin tractable as a closed scientific problem.

That honesty is not a gap to paper over. It is a **primary foundation** of biology and chemistry programs of this kind, including Corpus Nova.

## What this means for the program

1. **Map first, claim second.** We build a precise map of the living human organism — volumes, fluxes, receptors, hormones, mind–body links — without pretending we have solved how life began.
2. **Evidence standards stay strict.** Every number needs a source. Legend is labeled legend. Documented history is labeled documented. Unsourced rows stay `UNSOURCED`.
3. **Open questions stay visible.** Developmental biology, abiogenesis, first cells, and the jump from chemistry to organism remain marked as open where they are open — including the possibility that the right question has not been asked yet.
4. **Humility is operational.** White papers and map layers teach what is measured. They do not smuggle a fake origin story into the foundations.

## Relation to White Paper #1

**Corpus Nova White Paper #1** — *Soy, Estrogen and Exogenous Sex Steroids* (`white-papers/soy-estrogen-male-exogenous.md`) — is the program’s first white paper.
