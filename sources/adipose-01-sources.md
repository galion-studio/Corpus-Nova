# Sources: adipose-01 "How Fat Is Built"

Each citation was checked against PubMed (NCBI E-utilities) on 2026-11-01: authors, title, journal, year, volume, pages, DOI, and PMID. "Supports" lists which claim in the paper the source backs.

## Reviews (frame and background)

1. **Rosen ED, Spiegelman BM.** What we talk about when we talk about fat. *Cell.* 2014;156(1-2):20-44. doi:10.1016/j.cell.2013.12.012. PMID 24439368.
   *Supports:* overall framing of adipose tissue as an endocrine and metabolic organ; white, brown, and beige cell types; depot biology.

2. **Frayn KN.** Adipose tissue as a buffer for daily lipid flux. *Diabetologia.* 2002;45(9):1201-1210. doi:10.1007/s00125-002-0873-y. PMID 12242452.
   *Supports:* adipose tissue as a buffer that takes up fat after meals and releases it between them; insulin regulation of LPL and storage.

3. **Coleman RA, Lee DP.** Enzymes of triacylglycerol synthesis and their regulation. *Prog Lipid Res.* 2004;43(2):134-176. doi:10.1016/s0163-7827(03)00051-1. PMID 14654091.
   *Supports:* the glycerol-3-phosphate pathway (GPAT → AGPAT → lipin/PAP → DGAT) for building TAG.

4. **Walther TC, Farese RV Jr.** Lipid droplets and cellular lipid metabolism. *Annu Rev Biochem.* 2012;81:687-714. doi:10.1146/annurev-biochem-061009-102430. PMID 22524315.
   *Supports:* lipid droplet structure (neutral-lipid core with a phospholipid monolayer); droplets forming between the ER leaflets and budding off.

5. **Zechner R, Zimmermann R, Eichmann TO, Kohlwein SD, Haemmerle G, Lass A, Madeo F.** FAT SIGNALS—lipases and lipolysis in lipid metabolism and signaling. *Cell Metab.* 2012;15(3):279-291. doi:10.1016/j.cmet.2011.12.018. PMID 22405066.
   *Supports:* the ATGL → HSL → MGL lipolysis sequence.

6. **Goldberg IJ, Eckel RH, Abumrad NA.** Regulation of fatty acid uptake into tissues: lipoprotein lipase- and CD36-mediated pathways. *J Lipid Res.* 2009;50(Suppl):S86-S90. doi:10.1194/jlr.R800085-JLR200. PMID 19033209.
   *Supports:* LPL at the capillary releasing fatty acids from chylomicrons; CD36-assisted uptake into adipocytes.

7. **Rosen ED, MacDougald OA.** Adipocyte differentiation from the inside out. *Nat Rev Mol Cell Biol.* 2006;7(12):885-896. doi:10.1038/nrm2066. PMID 17139329.
   *Supports:* the transcriptional cascade of adipogenesis; the PPARγ–C/EBPα positive-feedback loop.

8. **Cannon B, Nedergaard J.** Brown adipose tissue: function and physiological significance. *Physiol Rev.* 2004;84(1):277-359. doi:10.1152/physrev.00015.2003. PMID 14715917.
   *Supports:* UCP1-mediated non-shivering heat production; brown fat as a cold-defense tissue.

9. **Sun K, Kusminski CM, Scherer PE.** Adipose tissue remodeling and obesity. *J Clin Invest.* 2011;121(6):2094-2101. doi:10.1172/JCI45887. PMID 21633177.
   *Supports:* very large adipocytes, low oxygen, inflammation, and fibrosis; the idea of healthy vs unhealthy expansion.

10. **Tchkonia T, Thomou T, Zhu Y, Karagiannides I, Pothoulakis C, Jensen MD, Kirkland JL.** Mechanisms and metabolic implications of regional differences among fat depots. *Cell Metab.* 2013;17(5):644-656. doi:10.1016/j.cmet.2013.03.008. PMID 23583168.
    *Supports:* depot-specific precursor behavior that persists in culture; subcutaneous vs visceral differences.

## Primary papers

11. **Hellerstein MK.** De novo lipogenesis in humans: metabolic and regulatory aspects. *Eur J Clin Nutr.* 1999;53(Suppl 1):S53-S65. doi:10.1038/sj.ejcn.1600744. PMID 10365981.
    *Supports:* de novo lipogenesis is a small contributor to fat stores on typical mixed diets. *(Strictly a review of the author's own isotope studies.)*

12. **Wilfling F, Wang H, Haas JT, Krahmer N, Gould TJ, Uchida A, et al.; Farese RV Jr, Walther TC.** Triacylglycerol synthesis enzymes mediate lipid droplet growth by relocalizing from the ER to lipid droplets. *Dev Cell.* 2013;24(4):384-399. doi:10.1016/j.devcel.2013.01.013. PMID 23415954.
    *Supports:* GPAT4 and DGAT2 moving onto droplets and growing them in place.

13. **Zimmermann R, Strauss JG, Haemmerle G, Schoiswohl G, Birner-Gruenberger R, Riederer M, et al.; Zechner R.** Fat mobilization in adipose tissue is promoted by adipose triglyceride lipase. *Science.* 2004;306(5700):1383-1386. doi:10.1126/science.1100747. PMID 15550674.
    *Supports:* the discovery of ATGL as the first-step TAG lipase.

14. **Tontonoz P, Hu E, Spiegelman BM.** Stimulation of adipogenesis in fibroblasts by PPAR gamma 2, a lipid-activated transcription factor. *Cell.* 1994;79(7):1147-1156. doi:10.1016/0092-8674(94)90006-x. PMID 8001151.
    *Supports:* PPARγ alone can push fibroblasts into becoming fat cells.

15. **Rosen ED, Sarraf P, Troy AE, Bradwin G, Moore K, Milstone DS, Spiegelman BM, Mortensen RM.** PPAR gamma is required for the differentiation of adipose tissue in vivo and in vitro. *Mol Cell.* 1999;4(4):611-617. doi:10.1016/s1097-2765(00)80211-7. PMID 10549292.
    *Supports:* fat cells cannot form without PPARγ.

16. **Spalding KL, Arner E, Westermark PO, Bernard S, Buchholz BA, Bergmann O, et al.; Arner P.** Dynamics of fat cell turnover in humans. *Nature.* 2008;453(7196):783-787. doi:10.1038/nature06902. PMID 18454136.
    *Supports:* adult fat-cell number is stable and mostly set by adolescence; about 10% of fat cells are renewed each year; carbon-14 birth dating.

17. **Wang QA, Tao C, Gupta RK, Scherer PE.** Tracking adipogenesis during white adipose tissue development, expansion and regeneration. *Nat Med.* 2013;19(10):1338-1344. doi:10.1038/nm.3324. PMID 23995282.
    *Supports:* the AdipoChaser lineage tracing; diet-induced adipogenesis mainly in visceral (epididymal) fat vs hypertrophy in subcutaneous fat in male mice.

18. **Jeffery E, Church CD, Holtrup B, Colman L, Rodeheffer MS.** Rapid depot-specific activation of adipocyte precursor cells at the onset of obesity. *Nat Cell Biol.* 2015;17(4):376-385. doi:10.1038/ncb3122. PMID 25730471.
    *Supports:* rapid, depot-specific precursor activation on a high-fat diet.

19. **Kim JY, van de Wall E, Laplante M, Azzara A, Trujillo ME, Hofmann SM, et al.; Scherer PE.** Obesity-associated improvements in metabolic profile through expansion of adipose tissue. *J Clin Invest.* 2007;117(9):2621-2637. doi:10.1172/JCI31021. PMID 17717599.
    *Supports:* healthy subcutaneous expansion (adiponectin-overexpressing ob/ob mice) improving metabolism despite more fat.

20. **Zhang Y, Proenca R, Maffei M, Barone M, Leopold L, Friedman JM.** Positional cloning of the mouse obese gene and its human homologue. *Nature.* 1994;372(6505):425-432. doi:10.1038/372425a0. PMID 7984236.
    *Supports:* leptin as a fat-derived hormone.

21. **Scherer PE, Williams S, Fogliano M, Baldini G, Lodish HF.** A novel serum protein similar to C1q, produced exclusively in adipocytes. *J Biol Chem.* 1995;270(45):26746-26749. doi:10.1074/jbc.270.45.26746. PMID 7592907.
    *Supports:* the discovery of adiponectin (Acrp30) as an adipocyte hormone.

22. **Fedorenko A, Lishko PV, Kirichok Y.** Mechanism of fatty-acid-dependent UCP1 uncoupling in brown fat mitochondria. *Cell.* 2012;151(2):400-413. doi:10.1016/j.cell.2012.09.010. PMID 23063128.
    *Supports:* UCP1 carrying protons with the help of fatty acids, shown by direct patch-clamp of the inner membrane.

23. **Cypess AM, Lehman S, Williams G, Tal I, Rodman D, Goldfine AB, et al.; Kahn CR.** Identification and importance of brown adipose tissue in adult humans. *N Engl J Med.* 2009;360(15):1509-1517. doi:10.1056/NEJMoa0810780. PMID 19357406.

24. **van Marken Lichtenbelt WD, Vanhommerig JW, Smulders NM, Drossaerts JM, Kemerink GJ, Bouvy ND, Schrauwen P, Teule GJ.** Cold-activated brown adipose tissue in healthy men. *N Engl J Med.* 2009;360(15):1500-1508. doi:10.1056/NEJMoa0808718. PMID 19357405.

25. **Virtanen KA, Lidell ME, Orava J, Heglind M, Westergren R, Niemi T, et al.; Nuutila P.** Functional brown adipose tissue in healthy adults. *N Engl J Med.* 2009;360(15):1518-1525. doi:10.1056/NEJMoa0808949. PMID 19357407.
    *23–25 together support:* active, cold-responsive brown fat in adult humans (neck, supraclavicular, and paraspinal regions), published in the same NEJM issue.

26. **Seale P, Bjork B, Yang W, Kajimura S, Chin S, Kuang S, et al.; Spiegelman BM.** PRDM16 controls a brown fat/skeletal muscle switch. *Nature.* 2008;454(7207):961-967. doi:10.1038/nature07182. PMID 18719582.
    *Supports:* classical brown fat shares a Myf5+ lineage with skeletal muscle; PRDM16 is the switch.

27. **Wu J, Boström P, Sparks LM, Ye L, Choi JH, Giang AH, et al.; Spiegelman BM.** Beige adipocytes are a distinct type of thermogenic fat cell in mouse and human. *Cell.* 2012;150(2):366-376. doi:10.1016/j.cell.2012.05.016. PMID 22796012.
    *Supports:* beige cells are a distinct cell type; adult human "brown" fat has beige-like gene features.

28. **Fox CS, Massaro JM, Hoffmann U, Pou KM, Maurovich-Horvat P, Liu CY, et al.; O'Donnell CJ.** Abdominal visceral and subcutaneous adipose tissue compartments: association with metabolic risk factors in the Framingham Heart Study. *Circulation.* 2007;116(1):39-48. doi:10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.106.675355. PMID 17576866.
    *Supports:* visceral fat is more strongly tied to metabolic risk factors than subcutaneous fat.

## General physiology numbers (textbook-level, no single primary source)
- Energy density: about 9 kcal/g for fat and about 4 kcal/g for carbohydrate or protein (Atwater factors).
- Glycogen is stored with about 2–3 g of water per gram, a range that is commonly cited and varies by method.
- Lean adult fat mass of about 10–15 kg, equal to roughly 100,000 kcal. This is an order-of-magnitude estimate.

## Sex differences & sex-hormone effects on fat distribution

25. **Karastergiou K, Smith SR, Greenberg AS, Fried SK.** Sex differences in human adipose tissues — the biology of pear shape. *Biol Sex Differ.* 2012;3:13. doi:10.1186/2042-6410-3-13. PMID 22651247.
    *Supports:* gynoid vs android patterns; gluteofemoral fat and metabolic protection; sex-steroid and intrinsic depot differences.

26. **Frank AP, de Souza Santos R, Palmer BF, Clegg DJ.** Determinants of body fat distribution in humans may provide insight about obesity-related health risks. *J Lipid Res.* 2019;60(10):1710-1719. doi:10.1194/jlr.R086975. PMID 30097511.
    *Supports:* estrogen favoring gluteofemoral SCAT and limiting VAT; menopause shift; testosterone and visceral fat in men; elevated androgens / central fat in women (e.g. PCOS context).

27. **Karastergiou K, Fried SK, Xie H, Lee MJ, Divoux A, Rosencrantz MA, Chang RJ, Smith SR.** Distinct developmental signatures of human abdominal and gluteal subcutaneous adipose tissue depots. *J Clin Endocrinol Metab.* 2013;98(1):362-371. doi:10.1210/jc.2012-2953. PMID 23150689.
    *Supports:* depot- and sex-dependent gene programs (incl. HOX) in abdominal vs gluteal SAT.
