## F. Map UI — reuse, don't rebuild

This paper supplies **payloads** (hover cards and library entries), not a viewer. For the body-map UI, reuse candidates surveyed in [`notes/github-anatomy-landscape.md`](../notes/github-anatomy-landscape.md):
- **ashemag/human-atlas** (BodyParts3D adult male; 2,234 meshes)
- **slorksmo/Human-Atlas** (matches the male + female sex-layer requirement; compare licence and mesh quality)
- **Z-Anatomy** (CC BY-SA 4.0)
- **HongChao6/open-anatomy-studio** (hotspot and lesson patterns for binding paper content to the map)
- **OPANEX**
- datasets: **BodyParts3D** and the **Human Reference Atlas**

Check every licence and attribution before adopting one.
