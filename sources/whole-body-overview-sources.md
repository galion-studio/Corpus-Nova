## Citation notes (v3)
- Female testosterone source split: Handelsman 2018 [18] gives roughly equal thirds; Burger 2002 [26] gives ~25/25/50. BioChem's cards show both; the paper says "roughly a third each" with both cited.
- Reed & Carr (Endotext, menstrual cycle) could not be verified (NCBI captcha, no Crossref match), so it is not cited. Cycle mechanics are cited to Guyton [7].
- Homologue pairs and the indifferent-gonad stage are cited to Gray's [4] and Guyton [7] (textbook-level facts).

## Notes for editors
- Numbers in the paper are rounded "reference adult" values; real people vary by sex, age, and size.
- Skin area given as a range (1.5–2 m²) rather than one reference value.
